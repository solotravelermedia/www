Solo Traveler Media

Empowering solo travelers through tools, content, and community.

Our Mission

Solo Traveler Media Group empowers and supports solo travelers around the world through inspiring content, practical tools, and community-driven resources. We advocate for fair treatment of solo travelers and aim to make solo travel more accessible, inclusive, and rewarding for everyone.

What We Do

Curated Content

From travel guides to safety tips and blog features, we provide resources tailored to solo travelers.

Tools & Services

We build practical tools including packing checklists, time zone converters, and trip planning platforms.

Community & Advocacy

We champion fair pricing and recognition for solo travelers across the travel industry.

Our Properties

Solo Traveler Blog

Open, community-driven initiative dedicated to supporting solo travelers with accessible tools, reliable information, curated resources, and travel intelligence designed for those exploring the world on their own.

Solo Travel Society

Community-driven network bringing solo travelers together to share experiences, discover new perspectives, exchange practical advice, and build meaningful connections with others who value the freedom, confidence, and independence of exploring the world on their own.
